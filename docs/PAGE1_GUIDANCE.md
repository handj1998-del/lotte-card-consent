## Clause-to-display inventory
- Purpose introduction identifies Lotte Rental Co., Ltd. as the Company.
- Five purposes: extra rental charges for longer use; car damage except full
  damage waiver coverage; speeding/traffic-law/illegal-parking/unauthorized
  disabled-parking fines; highway/road tolls during the rental; cleaning for
  prohibited conduct such as smoking.
- All collected items: renter name; contact information including phone/email;
  reservation number; card type; issuer; number; expiry.
- Retention: **90 days following return of the rental vehicle**, then secure
  destruction of all collected personal information. Not 90 days after signing,
  reservation or entry; not “up to 90 days”.
- Settlement: notice through supplied email OR phone with incurred charges;
  payment using supplied debit/credit information upon notification.
- Rights: refusal is permitted; services may be limited. Signature confirms
  understanding/agreement to collection and use as described.
- Acknowledgment: read/understood; Company identified; freely given consent
  without coercion; collection AND use preserved.
- Registration note: payment card owned by renter/driver only.
